AI code can take down production. Just not at Microsoft.

Take a small product. Two services, a deploy that takes five minutes, everyone pushes to main. One bad commit, from a person or an agent, and everything is down. That's real.

Now take a system with thousands of servers. A rollout takes hours and starts with a small share of users. Metrics watch every step, and a rollback is automatic. A bad commit dies on the first step.

That's why I don't believe "AI broke Outlook". Microsoft says it was a network capacity upgrade, and that fits. Outages of this size come from infrastructure, not from one bad commit.

The useful question is for the rest of us. Agents write more code every month. If one push can take your product down, the agent isn't your problem. Your path to production is.
