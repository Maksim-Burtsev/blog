To work well with agents, first learn how to manage yourself.

Smarter models won't do it for you. However good they get, five agents still bring back five results from five different tasks, and switching between them costs you the way it costs a processor.

The one thing that really worked for me is batching, in two places.

Decisions. I have sessions where the only thing I do is answer questions about future tasks. The agent brings them worked through and grouped by topic, a few dozen tasks in one sitting.

Tasks. Once they're fully prepared, I send them to agents all at once. They implement, test, verify, deploy and close them. I return only for a demo when the interface changed.

It's not a silver bullet. One thing at a time is simply how people work best.
