My talking speed is 2x my typing speed. But it didn't make me even 1.5x more productive.

For 90 days, I dictated 95% of my prompts to AI agents.

The speed did not shorten my prompts. I spent the same time on them or even more.

But I gave the agents more context so they ask less questions, finished tasks faster and the work came out better because they had more details.

So the win isn't in typing prompts faster. It's that more details up front mean fewer touches on every task.
