AI-written text is slop in any domain, and specs are no exception. After a quarter of SDD, I’m back to code.

SDD was supposed to mean reading less code. At first it worked: you read 300 lines of plain text about a feature, open the implementation, and see the same thing in code.

Then the volume hit. On a busy day, the team ships several features, and each one comes with a spec and its pull requests. That’s a chapter of a book to read, on top of the code.

After a couple dozen specs, you see it. Whatever the task, they read the same: the same structure, the same amount of text. It feels like a button color change would get a 100-line spec.

So I read the key points and open the code. The spec didn’t replace the review. It added a second one.
