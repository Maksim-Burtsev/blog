If someone sends me raw LLM text, I don't read it.

At work or with friends, it's the same. I say it directly: tell me in your own words, or we stop here. Usually that's where the topic ends.

The reason is density. Five or six paragraphs from a model almost always come down to two or three sentences. Rewriting them takes thirty seconds. You don't even have to type: speech-to-text is right there.

If someone didn't spend those thirty seconds on me, I know how they see our conversation.

And the rewrite helps the sender first. Two ideas are easier to hold than five paragraphs. Put them in your own words, and you find there was less in that text than it seemed. You also understand better what you're asking.
