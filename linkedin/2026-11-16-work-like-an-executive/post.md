Work with your coding agents the way people like Elon Musk work with their teams.

Running many agents in parallel and constantly switching context leaves you drained after a few hours.

I wondered how someone like Musk stays across the technical details of several companies. When your attention and decisions are the most valuable resource in a company, I figure a team of professionals works everything through in advance. They come with the question, the context, a few solutions, and the reasoning, so you can choose in minutes.

Agents are now good enough to be that team. Mine put prepared proposals with screenshots and diagrams into one decision desk where I only decide. In an hour, I make the decisions that used to take a whole evening.

Have your agents bring you worked-out options instead of managing a swarm of chats, and spend your energy on direction and the final call.
