When you only see a bug once to choose a fix and review the diff, the codebase stops living in your head.

Before, people answered questions about their part of the system without even viewing the code. Now engineers can less and less often explain the implementation in real time in a meeting or a chat. They need to ask their agent first to refresh their memory, becoming a proxy.

That makes communication between business and developers much slower, a side effect few people notice. We solved one bottleneck as agents reproduce bugs faster, and by winning there we have already paid for it.

Knowing a codebase comes from the number of touches you get while hunting a bug down and fixing it. Cut those touches down to reviewing a pull request diff (most of the time once), and you remember both the task and the system get worse and worse over time.
