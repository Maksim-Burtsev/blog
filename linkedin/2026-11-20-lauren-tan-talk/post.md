Lauren Tan at SpaceXSI shipped 3,000 pull requests to production last month with agents, and her talk "All Code Is Slop (Except Mine)" shows the only reason that volume is possible: when most of your token usage goes to testing and verification, the quality of what ships goes way up.

Almost nobody talks about this. The discussion goes to pull request size and AI slop. Respect to her for sharing her practice, ideas and skills openly.

We see the same on production systems with real users. The team ships more, and incidents and hotfixes after release dropped. Random breakage is rarer; remaining errors are mostly corner cases a few percent of users hit.

When agents do the work well and you invest heavily in testing and verification, the quality of what reaches production goes way up. That jump in quality is the only reason shipping at high volume works in practice.
