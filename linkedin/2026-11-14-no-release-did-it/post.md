No single release made agents work. Hundreds of small improvements did, and nobody noticed the moment they added up.

Every week something gets a little better: a new model, an update to the harness, one more feature. Each step is small, and you get used to it in a day.

This summer I left agents on long tasks and got a decent result. Done, but I checked everything and almost always fixed something.

This autumn, on routine tasks, I hand the work off and often don't open the chat again. Nothing big happened in between. The small steps crossed a line, and things started to just work.

I only saw it in my stats: every month, more chats I never came back to.

The normal level rises so fast that you only see it when you stop and look back.
