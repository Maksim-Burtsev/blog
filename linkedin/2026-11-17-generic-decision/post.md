On a key question, a generic decision made by an agent is worse than your own wrong one.

About 10% of the questions in any work are fundamental. For a product, that is the idea, the philosophy, or the key feature.

You can't just delegate that to AI, and it doesn't matter whether it's Astra, Fable or unreleased model that is beating everyone on benchmarks. It gives an average answer (because there’s a lot of guardrails, rules and etc.), and anyone who asks gets an identical one, but maybe in a different form, so you end up with the same solution as everyone else and aren't competitive.

That 10% needs full focus and deep work. It doesn't get decided in two minutes, and sometimes takes days, weeks or even months. Other times you simply don't know enough yet and need to pause and fill the gap before deciding.

You have to know exactly what you delegate and what you don't. Where a choice defines your product or business, a generic answer is unacceptable.
