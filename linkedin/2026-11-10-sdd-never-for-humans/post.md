You spent thousands of hours learning to read code. Spec-driven development asks you to read AI-written documents instead.

That's a bad deal. When you read code, syntax and patterns go by on autopilot. Your attention goes to what matters: the data, the business logic, what can break. That's why you can go through a big diff fast and still get the whole picture.

A spec switches all of that off. It's plain text, half business, half technical, and every line is supposed to matter.

Your experience only works on code. Use it there.
